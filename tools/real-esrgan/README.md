Local upscaling of images and video.

## What it is

A local upscaler that takes an image or a video and returns a larger one,
on the person's own machine. Nothing leaves the machine.

## The package

`source github.com/xinntao/Real-ESRGAN; licence BSD-3-Clause. Latest
release v0.3.0, last changed 2024-04-03.` Stated as what was handed over, nothing more.

## What it reads

An image or video file the person supplied, and local model weights.

## What it never does here

Called as a separate local program; none of its code goes into
the commons or a Recipe; nothing leaves the machine.

## Scope

Local upscaling of images and video.

## Its use here

The Marketing Delegate's video and image work. Not built or used yet.
